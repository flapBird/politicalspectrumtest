Explore Your Political Values

Four dimensions instead of one line — because "left" and "right" alone can't describe how you think about money, freedom, tradition, or the world.

Economy

How wealth should be created and shared — the role of markets, taxes, and the welfare state.

Economic leftEconomic right
Keynes believed markets need steering ↔ Friedman believed markets work best free.
Civil Liberties

Freedom versus order — privacy, speech, policing, and how much power the state should have.

Liberty firstOrder first
Jefferson put liberty first ↔ Hamilton put a strong union first.
Society

How fast society should change — family, faith, identity, and whose traditions set the rules.

Open to changeHold to tradition
Beauvoir believed society can be remade ↔ Burke believed institutions carry wisdom.
Global Outlook

Nation or world first — trade, borders, treaties, and how much sovereignty to pool.

One worldNation first
Kant imagined cooperation across borders ↔ de Gaulle put the nation first.

How the Test Works

01
Answer 24 statements

Plain-language questions about money, freedom, and the world. Strongly disagree to strongly agree — there are no right answers.

02
See your map instantly

Scores are computed in your browser the moment you finish. Nothing is uploaded, no account is needed, and you can pause anytime.

03
Compare and share

Get your closest ideology matches, then share a link that carries your scores — or download your map as an image.

Private by design. Your answers stay on your device — no accounts, no cookies, no tracking of what you clicked.
