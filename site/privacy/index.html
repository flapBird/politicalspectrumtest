Privacy

Your answers stay on your device

This page is short because we collect almost nothing. Reading it should take less time than the test.

The short version: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads, no server-side storage. The test’s scoring happens in your browser, and your answers never leave your device.

What we collect

Nothing. We don’t ask for your name, email, or any personal information, and we don’t transmit your answers or results anywhere. Like any website, our hosting provider may process standard technical request data (such as IP address, browser type, and requested page) for security and delivery — but we have no analytics and cannot identify who you are from that data.

Local storage on your device

To let you pause and resume, and to keep your last result, the test saves two entries in your browser’s local storage:

  • pst_progress_v1 — your current question position and answers, so you can continue later.
  • pst_result_v1 — your last four dimension scores.

These entries never leave your device and are not readable by other websites. You can erase them by clicking “Start over” on the test, clearing your browser’s site data, or using your browser’s “clear browsing data” option.

Share links

If you use “Copy result link” or “Share…”, the link contains only your four dimension scores (for example /results/?e=32&c=74&s=62&g=58). Your individual answers are never included. Anyone opening the link sees only what those four numbers encode.

Cookies, analytics, and ads

We use no cookies, no analytics or tracking scripts, no advertising, and no social-media widgets. If that ever changes, this page will be updated first — and any analytics would remain off for local development and testing environments.

Fonts and external requests

Fonts (Fraunces and Inter, both under the SIL Open Font License) are served directly from this website. The site makes no requests to third-party domains.
