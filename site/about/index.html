About the project

Understanding politics, one dimension at a time

Political Spectrum Test is a free, independent educational project. It helps people see where they — and the world’s political traditions — actually sit on the spectrum, with a transparent test and plain-English guides.

Why we built this

Most political quizzes compress politics into a single “left or right” score, bury the scoring logic in a black box, or monetize your answers. We think understanding your own position should be free, transparent, and private. So the test runs entirely in your browser, the scoring is published in full on the methodology page, and four dimensions carry more nuance than one line ever could.

Our principles

  • Free and open. No sign-up, no paywall, no ads. The guides are free to read; the test is free to take, retake, and share.
  • Non-partisan. We describe every tradition fairly — from democratic socialism to national conservatism — and we don’t rank them, endorse any, or accept political funding. “Neighbour on the spectrum” is a description, not a judgment.
  • Private. Answers never leave your device. There are no accounts, cookies, or analytics. Share links contain only your four scores.
  • Transparent. Every formula is published. If you disagree with a question or a reference position, you can see exactly how it affects results — and you’re welcome to tell us.
  • Educational. Results are a starting point for reflection and reading, not a scientific verdict or a political label.

What’s next

We’re expanding the ideology guide library, adding more countries’ party landscapes, and preparing translations beyond English. Longer term: a fuller questionnaire for people who want more depth, and country-comparison pages.

Contact

Questions, corrections, or suggestions are genuinely welcome — especially factual corrections to the guides, which we fix quickly. Write to hello@politicalspectrumtest.online.
