Economy

  1. The government should provide a strong safety net — healthcare, education, and housing support — even if that means higher taxes.
  2. Most industries work better when private companies compete than when the government runs them.
  3. Taxes on income and business should generally be lower so people keep more of what they earn.
  4. Large companies need strict regulation to protect workers, consumers, and the environment.
  5. It is unfair that the highest earners pay a much larger share of their income in taxes.
  6. Essential services like water, energy, and rail should mostly be owned or controlled by the state.

Civil Liberties

  1. People should be free to say offensive things in public, as long as they do not directly incite violence.
  2. Security agencies should have more power to monitor private communications to stop serious crime.
  3. The police should operate under strict limits and independent oversight, even when crime is a serious problem.
  4. During a serious crisis, governments should be allowed to temporarily restrict some civil liberties.
  5. Adults should be free to make any choice they want about their own body and health.
  6. Governments should be able to remove online content that spreads harmful misinformation.

Society

  1. Society should actively correct historic injustices, even if that means changing long-standing institutions.
  2. Traditional family structures are the foundation of a healthy society.
  3. Changing social norms around gender and relationships are mostly a good thing.
  4. Religion and faith communities deserve a central place in public life.
  5. Schools and public culture should reflect the full range of cultures and lifestyles in society, not just the majority’s.
  6. Society works best when everyone shares the same core customs and values.

Global Outlook

  1. Countries should accept less national sovereignty so international bodies can tackle problems like climate change.
  2. My country should put its own interests first, even when that means stepping back from international agreements.
  3. Freer trade between countries tends to benefit ordinary people, not just corporations.
  4. Immigration should be reduced significantly to protect national identity and local workers’ wages.
  5. Wealthy countries have a duty to help poorer countries develop, not just out of charity.
  6. Our borders and military should be strengthened because the world is becoming more dangerous.
